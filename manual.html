MANUAL.exe BLITZ SLAYER V2 — How To Play

PLAYER TUTORIAL

Use this guide anytime. If you opened this during battle, closing this tab returns you to the exact same game state.

1) Your Mission Objective

  • Destroy the AI’s hidden modules before it destroys yours.
  • After Round 30: Sudden Death triggers — losing a full module ends the game.

2) Round Rules 3 Actions

You get 3 Actions every round. Each card uses 1 Action.
Energy is required to play cards. Stronger cards cost more (max cost 4).
SKIP TURN ends your round early (but resets streak and reduces next-round energy).

3) Using Cards 3 Activation Types

TARGET ENEMY: Click card → click enemy grid.
TARGET SELF: Click card → click your grid.
INSTANT: Click card only (no targeting).

4) Card Types Legend

OFFENSE — direct damage.
INTEL — scan and reveal.
UTILITY — repairs and movement.
COUNTER — defense and turn control.
MALWARE — debuffs the AI for 1 round.

5) Streak Accuracy Chain

  • Streak increases only when your attack hits a real AI segment.
  • Intel / utility / malware do not increase streak.
  • Skipping resets streak.

7) Pro Tips Win More

  • Scan before bombing. Don’t waste energy early.
  • Use Trace Route to narrow search — then strike.
  • Patch damaged segments before the AI finishes the module.
  • Skip only when necessary — it costs energy and streak.
